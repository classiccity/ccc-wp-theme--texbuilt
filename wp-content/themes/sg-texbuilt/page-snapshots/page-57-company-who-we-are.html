Who We Are

Headquartered in Irving, TEXBUILT is a commercial general contractor built to serve Texas developers, regional owners, and statewide opportunities with speed, accountability, and disciplined execution.

Backed by decades of commercial construction experience and a proven leadership bench, TEXBUILT combines the strength of an established builder with the responsiveness of a Texas-focused team. We understand this market because we live and build here.

From retail to healthcare and industrial to multifamily, hospitality, entertainment, storage and mixed-use developments, TEXBUILT is built to deliver projects that strengthen Texas communities and move at the pace this market demands.
