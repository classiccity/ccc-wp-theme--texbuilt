Prequalification Requirements

Prequalification happens through Compass after you submit the trade partner intro form. Gather these documents now and the Compass prequalification takes about 15 minutes — and we'll do our internal review within one business day.

Before you apply

What you'll need

Two lists below: information you'll fill in directly, and documents you'll upload as PDFs. Have everything ready before starting and the application is short.

List 1

Information needed

You'll type these directly into the application form:

  • Legal company name (matching W-9)
  • State and federal tax ID numbers
  • D&B (Dun & Bradstreet) number
  • Trades performed (primary + up to 4 secondary)
  • Geographic service area (counties or metros)
  • Three reference projects from the last 3 years (owner, GC, project size, contact)
  • Insurance carrier and policy limits

List 2

Documents to upload

PDFs you'll attach during the application:

  • Current W-9
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI) — General Liability, Auto, Workers' Comp
  • OSHA 300A logs from the last 3 years
  • EMR (Experience Modification Rate) letter from your insurer
  • Written safety program
  • Drug and alcohol policy
  • Reviewed financial statements (past 2 years)
  • Trade-relevant licenses and certifications
  • Bonding capacity letter (if subject to bonding)
  • Sample subcontract or master service agreement (if you have one)
