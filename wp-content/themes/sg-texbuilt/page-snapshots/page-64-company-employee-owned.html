The structure

An ESOP, in plain English

An Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) holds shares of TEXBUILT in trust for every employee. Shares vest over time as you work here. When you retire, you cash out at the company's then-current valuation. The longer you're here, the more you own — and the more aligned your interests are with the company's long-term success.

No employee buys in. No employee owes anything. The ownership is part of the compensation, alongside salary and benefits.
