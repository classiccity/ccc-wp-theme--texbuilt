Why Texas only

Local accountability is the whole point

TEXBUILT exists because the best work happens when the team building it lives in the same market they're delivering for. Texas projects, Texas people, Texas accountability — start to finish.

The TEXBUILT Standard (TXS)

We believe great projects are built through accountability, trust, hard work, and people willing to step up when it matters most. Whether in the office, in the field, or in the communities we serve, we hold ourselves to a higher standard because how we build matters just as much as what we build.
