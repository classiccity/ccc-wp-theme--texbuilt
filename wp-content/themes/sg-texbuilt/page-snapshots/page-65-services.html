What we do

Services

TEXBUILT is built on early alignment, execution, and consistent communication from preconstruction through project completion. Focused exclusively on Texas projects, we combine operational planning, proactive project management, and field-driven leadership to keep clients informed, trade partners engaged, and projects moving forward. Our focus is simple: reduce uncertainty, strengthen accountability, and deliver a better construction experience for everyone involved.

Our approach

Three phases. One responsibility.

We don't hand off between teams. The same group of people that priced your project also builds it and walks it through warranty.

Phase 1

Preconstruction

TEXBUILT approaches preconstruction as a strategic planning process — not a budgeting exercise. We bring operational insight, trade partner collaboration, and real-time market awareness together early to help clients make informed decisions, reduce risk, and create alignment before construction begins.

  • Conceptual and detailed estimating
  • Constructability review and value engineering
  • Risk modeling and contingency justification
  • Subcontractor selection and bid leveling
  • GMP negotiation and contract structuring

Phase 2

Project Management

Our project management approach is built on accountability, transparency, and execution. TEXBUILT maintains proactive communication, disciplined coordination, and consistent client engagement throughout every phase of the project — ensuring stakeholders remain informed, aligned, and confident as decisions are made and work progresses.

  • Single point of contact for the owner
  • Decision-tracking and milestone reporting
  • Weekly owner reporting with photos and milestones
  • Change order discipline — surfaced early, documented, agreed
  • Punch list management and warranty closeout

Phase 3

Construction

TEXBUILT is field-driven at its core. We focus on building strong relationships with trade partners, maintaining an organized and accountable jobsite, and delivering consistent execution that protects schedule, quality, and the client experience from start to finish.

  • On-site superintendent leadership
  • Subcontractor scheduling and quality control
  • Safety program enforcement (TEXBUILT and OSHA standards)
  • Organized jobsite logistics and trade coordination
  • Consistent field execution that protects schedule and quality
